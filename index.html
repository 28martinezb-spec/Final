Brok M. Egg

BBB Workshop Productions

Information of the project

It is based a black yoshi egg. It can hold up to 12 nintendo switch 1 and 2 games.
It has a pole in the middle to keep the top and bottom connected. It looks like a century egg.
The egg is a very egg shaped egg and because of that its much cooler than any other nintendo switch game holders.
